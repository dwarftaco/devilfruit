Gomu Gomu no Mi

Mythical Zoan

This devil fruit grants the user's body the properties of rubber. Originally, the fruit was called Hito Hito no Mi, Model: Nika and classified as a Mythical Zoan-type fruit that allows one to transform into the legendary "Sun God" Nika (and gain his rubbery attributes), before being renamed and reclassified by the World Government to hide the truth. The user's body permanently adopts the properties of rubber: their body can stretch, bend, bounce, inflate, and twist. Additionally, they become almost completely immune to lightning and blunt damage, such as blunt weapons, cannonballs, and even bullets, all of which bounce straight off them
